2The mission

A long-held goal — the "original Holy Grail": get GPS location out of P25 radio transmissions for firefighter safety, with no dependence on a cooperating base radio handing over the data. This project is the independent, passive, off-air answer: any Phase-1 fleet, a $40–100 receiver, an offline map, nothing installed on the viewer.

P25 Tier 1 (TIA-102.BAJB) is the standardized GPS-over-P25 format — NMEA carried in data PDUs. That is the target; not Motorola's proprietary LRRP, not Tier 2. We decode data and voice, never trunking — the fleet is Phase-1 conventional (FDMA, single channel, voice on-channel, "the easy one").

4The GPS decode chain

Discriminator audio in, NMEA out. All in decode/ (numpy/scipy research engine) with a byte-equal stdlib twin in decode_pure/ for the box.

StageWhat happens
1 · TapKenwood NX-700 DB-25 pin 19 (DEO), radio set WIDE (external, no disassembly). DSP-routed flat detected-signal output. We only ever receive.
2 · Front endfrontend.py — OP25's exact rmsagc → c4fm RX de-emphasis filter. (A hand-rolled moving-average pre-filter was the early fault; OP25's real filter fixed it.)
3 · Capture48 kHz native = integer 10 samples/symbol at 4800 baud; cleaner than 44.1 k resampled. Enhancements off, no clip, ~2 s lead-in.
4 · DemodGPS path → fsk4_demod.py MMSE tracking loop (clean eye; force-locks on the payload, never needs the NID). NID/header path → fixed-timing demod (no loop) — see §6.
5 · SliceAdaptive 4-level kmeans, not fixed thresholds (levels can sit asymmetric).
6 · Frame syncframer.py — streaming sync on 0x5575F5FF77FF → NID (BCH(63,16,11)) → process_blocks. DUID 5 = LDU1, 10 = LDU2.
7 · FECpdu_blocks.py — 1/2 + 3/4 trellis/deinterleave + CRC, plus Viterbi on the rate-1/2 blocks (OP25's greedy decoder failed 4/12; Viterbi recovers 12/12 → the whole sentence).
8 · AcquireStrict NID-validated framer, then a fallback force-lock at the best-Hamming sync near the onset, validated by the payload itself ($PP25 ID + readable GGA).

Burst gate. A packet is a drop in the broadband discriminator noise (FM quieting). Open the expensive decode only while quieted, on a fixed-geometry window from the onset (a variable window regressed to 1–2/10; fixed = 10/10).

Ground truth = the base's RNDIS NMEA feed, diffed against every decode (position AND unit ID). The RNDIS 80…81 wrapper is base-local USB, never over-the-air — the OTA decode sees the P25 PDU. Proven: 10/10 bursts on a clean capture, position + unit ID exact.

5The sensitivity result

Front end settled: Behringer UFO202 USB line-in straight off the DEO (switch on LINE, not PHONO). The radio's internal 4.7 µF already AC-couples the DEO — it's literally a wire. A flat line input removes the mic path's eye-shaping + key-up DC transient (distortion, not noise — so it stacks with the soft-decision gain).

The sweep (STD 511 / O.153, scored vs the −90 dBm clean reference) is a clean monotonic digital cliff. The 5% crossing interpolates to ≈ −119.1 dBm — raw pre-FEC channel BER, matching commercial P25 symbol recovery.

6The voice decode — the day's breakthrough

The block. First OTA voice capture: the MMSE demod locked every frame sync (NAC decoded to the real 0x455) but every NID failed BCH. Diagnosis that cracked it — symbol crispness by region: sync 74% on-target, body 78%, but the NID only 29%. The NID is the one fuzzy region, right after each sync.

Root cause + fix. The MMSE timing loop over-corrects a clock that never drifts (48 kHz = exactly 10× 4800 baud), wobbling ~30 symbols after each sync — landing the fuzz on the NID. Fix: fixed-timing demod (upsample ×4, pick the crispest fixed sample phase, no tracking loop). Every keyup then → ~29 valid frames, NAC 0x455 every frame, clean alternating LDU1/LDU2. A radio expert's nudge — "any chance it's AMBE+2, we have the DVstick to fall back on" — is what drove looking below the vocoder, where the real bug was.

Extraction (decode/voice_ldu.py): for each LDU, a DSD-style sequential dibit Reader (status symbol every 36 dibits; status_count starts 21 at the body = 57 dibits in: sync 24 + NID 32 + 1 status) → Annex-H deinterleave (TIA-102.BABA iW/iX/iY/iZ tables) into imbe_fr[8][23], inter-frame data-dibit gaps [0,0,20,20,20,20,20,20,16]. Frames → voice/imbe_ota.c (mbelib, mbe_processImbe7200x4400Frame = the 144-bit over-air Frame/ECC path) → s16 PCM.

Result. On the 27 s / 3-keyup capture: 747 voice frames, NAC 0x455 every frame, and the decoded audio's energy envelope is three loud bursts with near-silent gaps — the exact shape of the phrase spoken three times. Speech structure, not a noise smear. Confirmed intelligible by ear. No DVstick needed for Phase-1 IMBE (patents expired → mbelib is legal); the DVstick stays as the sanity-check arbiter.

8The landmines (holes not to fall in twice)

  • Symbol recovery is solved — use the MMSE demod; a fixed-step slicer gives ~15% errors. But for the NID/header, use FIXED timing when fs is an integer multiple of the symbol rate — the MMSE loop hurts there. Diagnose demod health by region, not just by sync lock.
  • Key-up DC transient is a capture artifact, not a decode bug; the burst body is clean. Trim ~1.5 s of settling before scoring BER.
  • BER patterns: STD CAL is NOT clean (4.98% baked in — it calibrates the meter); STD 1011 is the 0% baseline; STD 511/O.153 is the sweep. Near the cliff, score vs the clean −90 reference, not self-consistency.
  • IMBE is not a separate obstacle — it's the full-rate mode of the AMBE+2 family. Phase-1 IMBE patents expired → mbelib is a legal software decode. AMBE+2 (Phase-2/DMR) remains encumbered → the only case the DVstick is needed.
  • mbelib has two IMBE paths: 88-bit already-de-FEC'd info bits (a .ambe container) → mbe_processImbe4400Data; the 144-bit over-air LDU frame → mbe_processImbe7200x4400Frame (does Golay/Hamming itself).
  • The individual ID is just the radio's own unit ID — not a PTT/periodic tag. A decoded burst gives two things to check: position AND unit ID. The voice link-control source ID is the join key to the position layer.
  • Validate against ground truth (the RNDIS NMEA), not guesses. Receive-only, always.

10For the record — how it happened

This was not a machine teaching itself in a vacuum. A radio expert with 37 years at the bench handed clean captures at calibrated levels, caught the errors, and — the nudge that cracked the voice half — looked at a stuck decode and said "any chance it's AMBE+2, we have the DVstick to fall back on," which sent the search below the vocoder where the real bug was. The crispness-by-region diagnosis and the MMSE-vs-fixed-timing fix came out of decades of intuition for how a signal degrades. He ran every capture, every level, every keyup; the code and the DSP were the AI's. Together, P25 was read off the air in about a day.

He called it his Riemann hypothesis — the thing he'd chased for years. The honest difference: the Riemann hypothesis is one person's monument; this is a thing firefighters will carry on a cheap box someday because he refused to let "the agency wasn't interested" be the end of it. Off the air, free, nobody's gatekeeper.
